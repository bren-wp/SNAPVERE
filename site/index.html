How it works

Press, drag, done.

Snapvere waits in your tray and stays out of the way. There’s no editor window to manage and nothing to sign in to — the whole tool lives on top of your screen for as long as you need it.

  1. 01
    PrtSc

    Press Print Screen

    The screen freezes instantly on the monitor under your cursor — open menus and tooltips included.

  2. 02

    Drag over what matters

    A magnifier loupe and live dimensions make pixel-perfect edges easy. Move and resize until it’s right.

  3. 03
    Ctrl+C

    Copy, save or mark it up

    Draw on the selection, then copy it to the clipboard, save it to your gallery, or save it as a PNG, JPG or WebP file.

Annotate

Mark it up right where you took it.

The toolbar opens under your selection, and everything you draw is flattened into the capture — including a pixelating blur that really hides what’s underneath.

  • Arrow

    Point straight at the thing that matters.

  • Rectangle

    Frame a button, a row or a whole region.

  • Ellipse

    Circle a detail without covering it.

  • Line

    Straight connectors and underlines.

  • Pen

    Freehand, for everything else.

  • Marker

    A translucent highlight over text.

  • Numbered steps

    Click, click, click — 1, 2, 3 in order.

  • Text

    Type a label right on the capture.

  • Blur / redact

    Pixelate passwords, emails and keys for good.

  • Colour & undo

    Any colour, an eyedropper, four sizes, undo and redo.

Record

Record the screen. Draw on it live.

Press Ctrl + Shift + R, drag out an area and hit Enter. Snapvere records straight to MP4 or GIF with your cursor, mic and system audio. Circle things while you talk — the toolbar itself is never captured.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your computer.

Your captures are ordinary files on your disk, indexed by a small local database. There’s no account, no cloud and no telemetry — and that’s the whole system.

No account

Download, install, capture. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

No telemetry

The app sends no analytics or usage data. Not anonymously, not “to improve the product”.

Offline by design

Capture, recording, annotation and the gallery all work without a network connection.

Redaction built in

Pixelate sensitive details in screenshots — and in recordings, while you record.

Planned Private share links. Unguessable addresses (~128 bits), an optional password and an expiry from one hour to never. Never public, never indexed by search engines — and off unless you use it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Read our privacy policy.

Is Snapvere free?

Yes. Snapvere is completely free: no account, no ads and no trial. It runs on Windows 10 and 11; a macOS build is in testing.

How do I take a screenshot with Snapvere?

Press PrtSc, drag over the area you want and draw on it if you like. Then press Ctrl + C to copy it, Ctrl + S to save it to your gallery, or choose Save as… to export it as a file.

Can Snapvere record my screen?

Yes. Press Ctrl + Shift + R, drag out an area or pick the whole screen, and press Enter. Snapvere records to MP4 (H.264) or GIF at up to 60 fps, with the mouse cursor, your microphone and system audio if you want them, and a pause button.

Can I draw on a recording while it runs?

Yes — the same tools you get on a screenshot. Arrows, shapes, pen, marker, numbered steps, text and blur, drawn live over the area you’re recording and baked into the video. The toolbar itself never shows up in the recording.

Can I play my recordings inside Snapvere?

Yes. Since version 0.2.2, clicking a recording in the gallery plays it right there, with seeking, volume, playback speed and full screen. Screenshots open in a viewer where you can zoom up to 800% and pan, and the arrow keys move between captures.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Snapvere preview builds are not digitally signed, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Download only from the official Snapvere download center, verify the SHA-256 checksum and only proceed if you trust the application.

Does Snapvere work on both Windows and Mac?

Snapvere runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) today. The macOS build for Apple Silicon and Intel is in testing and goes up on the download page as soon as it’s ready.

What makes Snapvere more private than other screenshot tools?

Nothing leaves your computer. There’s no account and no telemetry, the app works fully offline, and blur / redact is a first-class tool. Private share links — unguessable addresses, an optional password and an expiry — are planned, and will never be indexed by search engines.
